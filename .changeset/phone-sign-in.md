Phone sign-in: a `phone` field type and `phone`/`phoneVerified` system fields on auth collections, and, for collections with `phoneSignIn`, sign-in and sign-up with codes texted through the new `sms` option, phone and password sign-in, password reset by SMS, and phone verification and change. Codes are six digits, expire after 10 minutes and work once; texts are limited per phone and optionally to a list of countries.
